# Project Brief

## Overview

This project involves designing a Hanukkah menorah in the shape of an eight-sided die (an octahedron). Because the heat of the candles would melt plastic, the menorah will be milled out of aluminum plate and aluminum bar stock.

## Project Goals

1. Design an eight-sided die (octahedron) where each face is a triangle.
2. Each of the 8 faces will have a specific number of candle holders (from 1 to 8) arranged in a circle in the wider, bottom half of the triangle.
3. Each face will also have a single "starter" candle (shamash) holder near the top point of the triangle.
4. The starter candle holder will potentially serve a dual purpose: holding the starter candle and acting as a structural connector to hold the sides of the die together.
5. The design must be suitable for CNC milling from aluminum stock.

## Key Design Considerations

- **Material:** Aluminum plate (0.05 inches thick) for the sides, and aluminum square bar stock (0.75 inch square for the candle holders, 1.5 inch square for the equator connectors, 2 inch square for the apex connectors).
- **Candle Holders:** Milled from 0.75 inch lengths of the 0.75 inch square bar stock. They will have a central cylinder milled out to a depth of 0.6 inches to hold candles with a nominal 8.5 mm base diameter.
- **Assembly:** The candle holders will be attached to the aluminum plates using M3 flat head screws at each corner of the square holder.
- **Stock Dimensions:** The 8 triangular faces will be cut from 12×12 inch squares of 0.05 inch aluminum plate, each square halved diagonally (one half per face).
